I set out to paint a space scene, with some art from Starfield as reference, to practise space backgrounds and a light effect. It turned out very different: while painting, I thought of Carcosa from The King in Yellow in the middle of it, and a wanderer inching towards it. The stars are white gouache, spattered, and I went a bit overboard. I think it turned out pretty good.
